DoReChoir

Everything a choir needs to know, in one place, on every singer's phone.

DoReChoir brings the schedule, the concert projects and the day-to-day practicalities together, so singers find what they need and the board doesn't have to chase information.

Visit the DoReChoir app

For singers

  • A personal start page showing what is next and what has changed.
  • Calendar of rehearsals, concerts and other events.
  • Projects with participants, repertoire and what to wear.
  • Report absence, carpool and coffee duty, with swaps, in a couple of taps.
  • Reminders by push notification or e-mail, at most one e-mail a day.

For the board

  • See who is coming to a rehearsal at a glance.
  • Set up projects and concerts once; coffee lists and carpools follow automatically.
  • A list of open matters, so nothing falls between the chairs.
  • Voice leaders, the music librarian and the costume committee edit their own parts.

Built with care

  • Made for the phone: add it to the home screen like an app.
  • Accessible: meets WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, with a dark mode.
  • Private by design: personal details are only visible to the member and admins.
